# Roadmap
1. v0.1.0-alpha: portable C++ foundation, Qt shell, deterministic simulation and CI.
2. v0.2.0: dynamic PPI, validated synthetic scenarios, antenna and signal models.
3. v0.3.0: receive-only ADS-B adapters and provenance-aware track display.
4. v0.4.0: receive-only SDR spectrum and I/Q research toolkit.
5. v0.5.0: quantified tracking and anomaly research.
6. v0.6.0: geodesy, weather and flight research reports.
7. v1.0.0: documented, tested desktop research release.
All stages are proposed; do not claim functionality without recorded tests.
